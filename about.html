About FrameX

A marketplace that brings local photo-frame shops and the people who need frames together.

What is FrameX?

FrameX is a photo-frame marketplace. Instead of one shop, it is built to list frames from several local shops in one place, so you can compare styles and sizes and see who you are buying from.

The problem we want to solve

Finding the right frame usually means visiting shop after shop, or ordering from a large online store where the frame isn't made near you. FrameX aims to make local frame shops easy to find and browse online.

Why use FrameX

Local shops in one place

Browse frames from different shops and always see which shop each frame comes from.

Clear sizes and materials

Each product lists the sizes and material the shop provides, so you know what you are choosing.

Pickup or delivery

Each shop chooses what it offers. You see the options on the shop page before you decide.

How the local shop marketplace works

  1. Shops list their frames, sizes and prices.
  2. You browse, pick a size and build a cart.
  3. You send your order on WhatsApp; the shop prepares it for pickup or delivery.

Online checkout and payment: coming soon

Quality and customization

Every shop sets its own materials, finishes and sizes, shown on each product page. On a product page you can already choose a frame colour and size and preview your own photo inside that frame. Sending your photo with your order online and custom sizes are coming soon.
