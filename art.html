Custom paintings

How a custom painting works

You pay nothing to ask. Money is only taken in two steps, and only after the artist has said yes.

  1. 1. Choose and ask

    Pick an artist, a size and a type at a fixed price. Add your reference photo and send the request.

  2. 2. The artist answers

    They accept or decline. If they decline, nothing is charged and the request closes.

  3. 3. Pay the advance

    Once accepted, you pay the advance and the artist starts your painting.

  4. 4. Pay the rest, then delivery

    When the painting is finished you pay the remaining amount, and it is sent to you.
