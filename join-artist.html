For artists

Join FrameX as an artist

Show your finished work and take custom painting requests at prices you set. FrameX reviews every application.

Send your application

What you get

  • Your own profile and gallery. FrameX reviews each artwork before it is shown.
  • You decide. Accept or decline every custom request.
  • An advance before you start. The customer pays part of the price before you begin, and the rest before you send it.
  • Your address stays private. Customers see your city, never your street.

How it works

  1. Send your applicationFill in the form. This is a request, not an account.
  2. FrameX looks at your workWe check your details and your work, and contact you by phone or email.
  3. Set your passwordWhen you're approved, we send you a link to set your own password.
  4. Add your workLog in to your artist dashboard and add your artworks and custom painting prices.
