Memories deserve more than a place in a phone gallery.

FrameX is a project built around a simple idea: the photos that matter most should be somewhere you can actually see them.

Why FrameX exists

Most of our favourite photos live on a phone, scrolled past and rarely looked at twice. At the same time, a good photo frame — the kind that actually looks considered on a wall — usually means visiting shop after shop, or guessing at an online listing with no real sense of size or finish.

FrameX exists to close that gap: a place to browse frames from real local shops, understand exactly what you're choosing, and get a photo onto a wall instead of leaving it on a screen.

Our philosophy

Show, don't guess

A frame should be easy to picture before you buy it — the size, the colour, the finish. That's the whole idea behind the customization preview on every product page.

Real shops, named clearly

Every frame on FrameX is sold by a shop you can see by name, not an anonymous listing. We think that matters.

Honest about what's ready

Where something isn't built yet — online checkout, photo uploads to a shop — we say so, instead of pretending it works.

How we think about memories

A photo on a wall gets looked at differently than one in a camera roll. It becomes part of a room — something a guest notices, something you walk past every day. That's the shift FrameX is built around: turning a photograph into a physical, lasting object.

Our approach to frames

We don't believe a frame has to look generic to be affordable, or complicated to be customizable. Every product on FrameX shows its real size options, its available finishes, and which local shop makes it — so choosing a frame feels as considered as the photo going inside it.

Our team

FrameX is a project built around a simple idea — memories deserve more than a place in a phone gallery. There's no large team to introduce yet; as the marketplace grows with real shops and shop owners, this page will grow with it.
