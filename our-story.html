Memories deserve more than a place in a phone gallery.

FrameX is a project built around a simple idea: the photos that matter most should be somewhere you can actually see them.

  1. 01Where it begins

    A photo nobody sees

    Think of your favourite photo. A wedding day, a grandparent's smile, a child's first steps. There is a good chance it lives on a phone, somewhere between a screenshot and a grocery list, and that you haven't looked at it in months.

    We take more photos than any generation before us, and we look at them less. The moments that matter most end up being the easiest to scroll past. That felt wrong to us, and it is the reason FrameX exists.

  2. 02The other half

    The frame shop down the road

    Putting a photo on a wall should be simple. In practice it usually means going from shop to shop to see what each one makes, or ordering from a faraway listing and guessing at the size and finish.

    Meanwhile, local frame shops do careful, skilled work that is hard to discover unless you happen to walk past. The people who want a good frame and the people who make one are often only a few streets apart, and still don't find each other.

  3. 03The idea

    So we built a bridge

    FrameX brings the two together. It is a single place to browse frames from local shops, with every frame shown under the name of the shop that sells it.

    And because a frame is hard to imagine from a thumbnail, we used technology where it actually helps: choose a size and see how big it really is, switch frame colours, and place your own photo inside that exact frame before you decide. The frame you preview is the frame of that product, never a generic stand-in.

  4. 04Today

    An honest beginning

    FrameX is at the start of its story, and we would rather say so than pretend otherwise. Today you can explore frames, compare sizes and finishes, preview your own photo and build a cart.

    Online checkout and payment are still to come, so for now orders are arranged directly with us on WhatsApp or by email. Shops are added one at a time, as they join.

  5. 05What comes next

    More walls, more memories

    The next chapters are about making the whole journey work end to end: ordering from the shop you choose, sending your photo with the order, and collecting your frame or having it delivered.

    The goal stays the same throughout. Take a photo that deserves better than a phone gallery, and help it become something you walk past, and smile at, every day.

Our philosophy

Show, don't guess

A frame should be easy to picture before you buy it — the size, the colour, the finish. That's the whole idea behind the customization preview on every product page.

Real shops, named clearly

Every frame on FrameX is sold by a shop you can see by name, not an anonymous listing. We think that matters.

Honest about what's ready

Where something isn't built yet — online checkout, photo uploads to a shop — we say so, instead of pretending it works.

The people behind it

There is no large team to introduce yet. As the marketplace grows with real shops and shop owners, this page will grow with it.
