Important: this version of the FrameX website does not have a production backend or database. It does not currently send information to a server. This notice describes what the site does today, and what a future, fully connected version is expected to handle.

1. Information we may handle

Depending on how you use the site, this may include: your name, email address and phone number (if you use the Contact form); your address and order information (once ordering is connected); a photo you choose for a product that is made from your own photo; general device and browser information (such as screen size, used to make the site responsive); small amounts of data saved in your browser's local storage, such as your wishlist and saved designs; and, if you have an account, the contents of your cart.

2. Why we collect it

Information is collected only to provide the features you use: responding to a message you send, keeping your cart with your account so it is there on any device, and — in future — processing and delivering an order. We don't collect information for unrelated purposes.

3. How information may be used

Today: contact-form details are used only to reply to your message, once a messaging service is connected (see below). In future, with a backend connected, order and account information would be used to process orders, communicate with you about them, and coordinate pickup or delivery with the relevant shop.

4. What this site stores on your device

  • Your wishlist and saved designs are kept in your browser's local storage, on your device only. They are not sent to a server and are not visible to FrameX or any shop.
  • Your cart is not stored on your device. It is kept with your FrameX account (see section 9). While you are logging in to add an item, that one item waits in your browser tab and is removed as soon as it has been added or you close the login.
  • Photos you choose for a product that is made from your own photo are kept in your browser's storage (the file you chose and a small preview copy), so your choice survives a page reload or a visit to the login page. They leave your device only when you are logged in to a FrameX account: see “Your photos” in section 9. A photo that nothing on this site uses any more is removed from your browser's storage automatically.
  • Your camera (“View on My Wall”). When you press “View on My Wall” and allow it, your camera's live picture is shown on your own screen so that you can see a frame on your wall. It is not recorded and it is not sent to FrameX, a shop or anyone else, and the camera is switched off when you close the view. “Save photo” makes a picture file on your own device only; FrameX does not receive it. Where your phone offers them, its motion sensor and its AR feature are used in the same way: on your device, and only while the view is open.
  • The contact form does not submit to a server. If an email address is configured for FrameX, submitting the form opens your own email app with the message pre-filled, for you to send yourself — FrameX does not receive it automatically.
  • Your analytics choice. When you answer the analytics question (see below), the answer is kept in your browser's local storage so that you are not asked again. If you allow analytics, your browser also keeps a random visitor number and a visit number. If you say no, neither is created.

Analytics: only if you allow it

The first time you visit, FrameX asks whether it may count visits. Until you answer, and if you answer “No thanks”, nothing is counted and no visitor number is made. If your browser sends a “Do Not Track” or “Global Privacy Control” signal, we treat that as a no and do not ask.

If you allow it, we record: which pages are opened (the page's address without anything after “?”), which products, categories, templates, artworks, artists and shops are looked at, what is searched for (anything that looks like an email address or a phone number is removed first), that a checkout was started, the name of the website a visit came from, whether a phone, tablet or computer was used, and whether someone was logged in at the time (not who).

We do not record: your name, email address, phone number, postal address or account, your network (IP) address, your photos, or anything about your payment. These visitor records are deleted after a set time: about 13 months, unless FrameX has set a different one.

Google Analytics. Where FrameX has switched on Google Analytics for this site, the same events are also sent to Google after you allow analytics, and only then is Google's script loaded. Google sets its own cookies (their names start with “_ga”) and, like any website you connect to, sees your network address. Advertising features are switched off. If you later say no, FrameX removes those cookies and stops sending.

Not analytics: your orders, payments and account (section 9) are records FrameX needs to run the shop. They exist whether or not you allow analytics, and they are not sent to Google.

Analytics is OFF: you have not been asked yet.

5. Data security

Because this version of the site does not transmit personal information to a server, there is no FrameX-operated database to secure yet. Once a backend is connected, this notice will be updated to describe the security measures in place, such as encrypted connections and access controls.

6. Data retention

Local-storage data (wishlist, saved designs) stays on your device until you clear it yourself, for example by clearing your browser's site data. FrameX has no way to delete it remotely because it is never sent to us. Your cart is kept with your account until you remove the items or your account is deleted.

7. Third-party services

The site loads fonts from Google Fonts. If you grant permission, it uses your browser’s built-in location feature to find nearby shops. Where FrameX accounts are not switched on, the distance is worked out on your device and your coordinates are not sent to FrameX. Where they are switched on, your coordinates are sent to the FrameX server only to calculate distances and are not stored in the FrameX database (see section 9). No advertising trackers are used. Analytics is used only if you allow it: see “Analytics: only if you allow it” above.

8. Your rights

Since this version of the site does not store your personal data on a server, there is currently nothing held by FrameX to request, correct or delete beyond what's in your own browser, which you control directly. Once accounts and a backend are introduced, this section will describe how to access, correct or delete your stored information.

9. Accounts and nearby-shop search

Where FrameX accounts are switched on, the site talks to a FrameX server, and the following applies in addition to the sections above.

  • Your account. If you create an account, FrameX stores your name, email address and phone number (if you give one). Your password is stored only as a one-way hash; FrameX staff cannot read it. You can see and edit your details on your account page.
  • Login records. To notice someone guessing passwords, FrameX keeps a record of each login and each failed login: the time, the account it was aimed at, the reason it failed and the network address it came from. What was typed is never stored. These records are deleted after the same limited time as the visitor records above.
  • Who at FrameX can see your details. Only FrameX administrators, to help with an order or a problem: your name, contact details, saved addresses and order history. Each time an administrator opens a customer's record, that is written to a log.
  • Your orders and addresses. When you check out, FrameX stores the delivery addresses you save and, for each order, what you bought, the price you paid, the delivery address and contact details you gave, and the order's history. Orders are kept as business records and are shared with the shop that fulfils them. We email you about your order (placed, payment, shipped, delivered, cancelled, refunds) through our email delivery provider.
  • Payments. Online payments are handled by a payment gateway (Cashfree Payments) in its own secure window. Your card number, CVV, UPI PIN and bank login go to the gateway, not to FrameX, and FrameX never stores them. To open the payment the gateway receives the amount, your order number and your name, email and phone number. FrameX keeps only the gateway's payment reference, the amount, the method used (for example “UPI” or “card”) and whether the payment succeeded, failed or was refunded.
  • Your cart. You can browse without an account. To add something to a cart you log in, and the cart is then stored with your account on the FrameX server: the products, the options and quantity you chose, any note you wrote and, for a product made from your own photo (a photo frame, a FrameX Studio design, a wall-art set), the design's settings, its text, a small preview picture and the photos you uploaded for it (see “Your photos” below). Prices in your cart are worked out by the server from the catalogue. Only you can see or change your cart.
  • Your photos. A product that is made from your own photo needs that photo to be printed. When you are logged in and choose a photo, or when you log in to order, the original file is uploaded to the FrameX server and stored exactly as you sent it: it is not resized, re-compressed, enhanced or edited. With it FrameX keeps the file's name, its type, its size in pixels and its file size. Your photos are private. They have no public address and are only sent through links that work for a few minutes, to you, to FrameX staff handling your order, and to the shop that makes the item the photo belongs to. Another shop or another customer cannot open them, and each download by FrameX staff or a shop is recorded. A photo that is part of an order is kept with that order as part of its record. A photo that never becomes part of an order, and is not in your cart, is deleted automatically after about 30 days. Your photos are used only to make your order.
  • Staying logged in. A session cookie keeps you logged in. It contains a random code, not your password, and cannot be read by scripts on the page. Logging out ends the session.
  • Nearby shops. If you press “Use my location”, your browser asks your permission. Your coordinates are then sent to the FrameX server only to find shops near you and calculate their distance. They are not saved with your account or stored in the FrameX database.
  • Typing a location. If you enter a city, area or PIN code instead, that text is looked up through OpenStreetMap's Nominatim place search to find its approximate position.
  • Shop applications. Details sent through “Partner With FrameX” are stored so FrameX can review the shop and contact its owner.
  • Password codes by text message. If you reset your password with your mobile number, FrameX sends a one-time code to that number through an SMS delivery provider.
  • Password emails. If you ask to reset your password by email, FrameX sends a one-time code and link to the address on your account through an email delivery provider.

To have your account, an application or a photo you uploaded removed, contact us using the details below.
