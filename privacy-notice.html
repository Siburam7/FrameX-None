Last updated: October 2026

Important: this version of the FrameX website does not have a production backend or database. It does not currently send information to a server. This notice describes what the site does today, and what a future, fully connected version is expected to handle.

1. Information we may handle

Depending on how you use the site, this may include: your name, email address and phone number (if you use the Contact form); your address and order information (once ordering is connected); a photo you choose to preview inside a frame; general device and browser information (such as screen size, used to make the site responsive); and small amounts of data saved in your browser's local storage, such as your cart and wishlist.

2. Why we collect it

Information is collected only to provide the features you use: responding to a message you send, remembering what's in your cart between visits, and — in future — processing and delivering an order. We don't collect information for unrelated purposes.

3. How information may be used

Today: contact-form details are used only to reply to your message, once a messaging service is connected (see below). In future, with a backend connected, order and account information would be used to process orders, communicate with you about them, and coordinate pickup or delivery with the relevant shop.

4. What this site stores on your device

  • Your cart and wishlist are saved in your browser's local storage, on your device only. They are not sent to a server and are not visible to FrameX or any shop.
  • Photo previews you choose on a product page stay in your browser's memory for that page view and are discarded when you leave or refresh the page. They are not uploaded anywhere.
  • The contact form does not submit to a server. If an email address is configured for FrameX, submitting the form opens your own email app with the message pre-filled, for you to send yourself — FrameX does not receive it automatically.

5. Data security

Because this version of the site does not transmit personal information to a server, there is no FrameX-operated database to secure yet. Once a backend is connected, this notice will be updated to describe the security measures in place, such as encrypted connections and access controls.

6. Data retention

Local-storage data (cart, wishlist) stays on your device until you clear it yourself, for example by clearing your browser's site data. FrameX has no way to delete it remotely because it is never sent to us.

7. Third-party services

The site loads fonts from Google Fonts and, if you grant permission, uses your browser's built-in location feature to calculate distance to shops entirely on your device — your coordinates are not sent to FrameX. No advertising or analytics trackers are used in this version of the site.

8. Your rights

Since this version of the site does not store your personal data on a server, there is currently nothing held by FrameX to request, correct or delete beyond what's in your own browser, which you control directly. Once accounts and a backend are introduced, this section will describe how to access, correct or delete your stored information.

9. Contact us

Questions about this notice can be sent through the Contact page, by email to support.framex@gmail.com, or on WhatsApp at +91 93371 69824.
