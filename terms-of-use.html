3. Your photos

A product that is made from your own photo (a photo frame, a FrameX Studio design, a wall-art set, a template) can only be ordered with that photo: the product page asks for it before you can add the item to your cart or buy it, and a product that needs several photos needs all of them.

Your uploaded image is printed in the same original quality you provide. FrameX does not artificially enhance or improve the image quality. The file is stored exactly as you uploaded it and is not resized, sharpened or retouched. For the best print result, upload a high-quality image: FrameX and the shop are not responsible for a print that looks soft, dark or pixelated because the photo itself was.

The pictures shown on product pages and in previews are samples and on-screen approximations. Where a page lets you position or crop your photo, the print follows the position you chose as closely as the frame allows.

Your photo is uploaded when you order (or as soon as you choose it, if you are logged in), is kept private, and is shared only with FrameX and the shop that makes your order. See the Privacy Notice for how it is stored and for how long.

5. Orders

You can browse without an account. To add items to a cart and to order you log in; your cart is saved with your FrameX account. A cart is not an order.

Placing an order. At checkout you give a delivery address and choose a way to pay. The prices, any delivery charge, tax or Cash on Delivery fee and the total are calculated by FrameX and shown before you pay or place the order. Prices and availability are checked again at that moment; if something changed, you are shown the new total first.

Paying. Online payments are made through a payment gateway in its own secure window. FrameX does not see or store your card number, CVV or UPI PIN. An order paid online is placed only after the payment gateway has confirmed the payment; until then it is held for a short time and you can retry the payment. Cash on Delivery, where offered, is paid in cash when the order is delivered.

Cancelling and refunds. You can cancel an order from its page before production begins. If you paid online, the refund is sent to the payment method you used; how quickly it appears depends on your bank or payment provider. FrameX or the shop may cancel an order that cannot be fulfilled, with a full refund of anything paid.

Personalised products. The photos for a personalised product are uploaded with your order (see section 3). Because it is made for you, a personalised product can be cancelled only before production begins.

Gift wrapping. At checkout you can ask for your order to be gift wrapped, where every item in it can be. The charge is shown as its own line in the price before you pay or place the order. If an order that was gift wrapped is cancelled and refunded in full, the gift-wrapping charge is refunded with it.

6. Pricing

Prices shown are set by each shop and are displayed in Indian Rupees (INR). Prices may include size-based adjustments shown on the product page. Any delivery charge, tax, gift-wrapping charge and Cash on Delivery fee is calculated at checkout and shown before you pay or place the order.

7. Payments

Where online payment is switched on, payments are made through a licensed payment gateway (Cashfree Payments) in its own secure window. FrameX never sees or stores your card number, CVV or UPI PIN, and an order is treated as paid only when the gateway has confirmed the payment. Where online payment is not switched on, checkout offers Cash on Delivery only, where that is available. While checkout shows “Test mode”, online payments are not real and no money is taken.

8. Custom products
